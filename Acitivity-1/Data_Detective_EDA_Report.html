6. Three Primary Data Detective Findings

Finding 1: Severe Domestic Market Vulnerability
High Concentration Risk

Observed Evidence: The United Kingdom represents 84.6% (£9.03M) of gross business revenues. The single top foreign market (Netherlands) represents only 2.7% (£285k). Over 90.4% of registered customers reside in the UK.

Business Implication: The retailer possesses zero macroeconomic insulation. Any localized UK supply chain interruption, domestic inflation spike, or postal logistics strike threatens commercial solvency.

Finding 2: Q4 Holiday Seasonality Surge
Commercial Driver

Observed Evidence: Revenues rise steeply from September (£1.06M), escalating to October (£1.15M), and culminating in November at £1.51M. Q4 accounts for 34.9% of annual sales in under three calendar months.

Business Implication: The enterprise is fundamentally seasonal. Working capital, supplier bulk agreements, and temporary warehouse fulfillment staffing must be secured well in advance of September.

Finding 3: Extreme Customer Pareto Power-Law
Retention Leverage

Observed Evidence: The top 20% of customer accounts (867 clients) generate 74.6% (£6.65M) of sales. Customer #14646 alone accounts for £280,206 (2.6% of the company's annual revenue).

Business Implication: Churn among the top 100 enterprise accounts would devastate operating profit. High-touch retention delivers far higher ROI than broad customer acquisition.

8. Research Limitations & Analytical Caveats

  • Incomplete Year-End Boundary: The dataset concludes on December 9, 2011, meaning December 2011 figures cannot be directly compared to full calendar months.
  • Unattributed Guest Checkouts: 24.93% of orders lack a CustomerID, which may introduce moderate survivorship bias into lifetime value metrics.
  • Absence of Cost & Margin Data: Only selling prices (UnitPrice) are recorded. True profitability cannot be established without COGS (Cost of Goods Sold).
  • Single-Year Sample: Multi-year longitudinal trends cannot be validated; year-over-year seasonality is assumed from Q4 2010 vs Q4 2011 continuity.
